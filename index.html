FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find Answers to Your Questions

What are the admission requirements?

+

To enroll in SkillBloom courses, you need to be at least 18 years old or have parental consent. No prior experience is required for beginner courses. Simply fill out our admission form and we'll guide you through the enrollment process!

How long are the courses?

+

Course duration varies by level: Beginner courses are 3-4 weeks, Intermediate courses are 6-8 weeks, and Master Class programs are 12-16 weeks. Each course includes hands-on training and practical projects.

Do you provide certificates?

+

Yes! Upon successful completion of any course, you'll receive a recognized SkillBloom Certificate. Our certificates are respected in the industry and can be added to your resume or LinkedIn profile.

What payment options do you offer?

+

We accept multiple payment methods including bank transfers, credit/debit cards, and mobile money. We also offer flexible payment plans for longer courses. Contact us for more details on payment options.

Can I transfer between courses?

+

Yes, we understand that interests may change. You can transfer to a different course within 2 weeks of enrollment. Contact our admin team to discuss transfer options and any applicable fees.

Is there job placement assistance?

+

Yes! Our career services team assists graduates with job placements. We have partnerships with various companies and regularly share job opportunities with our alumni network.

Do you offer online courses?

+

While our primary focus is hands-on in-person training, we do offer some introductory modules online. Contact us to discuss which courses may have hybrid or online options available.

What if I miss a class?

+

We understand life happens! You can attend make-up sessions or request recorded materials from the class you missed. Our instructors are supportive and will help you catch up.
